\makeabstract
{
Impact of Structured Mentoring Practices on Undergraduate Summer Research Experience
}
{
Julia Aram (1), Chloe Kouzoudjian (1), Wasifa Orthy (1), Vonna Hemmler, Ph.D. (1), Kate Follette, Ph.D. (1)
}
{
\textsuperscript{1}Amherst College 
}
{
Effective mentorship is essential for student success in undergraduate research experiences. However, specific mentoring practices that have been shown to be effective are currently under-researched, particularly in STEM programs. The Facilitating Undergraduate Research Through Holistic and Extensive Skills Training (FURTHEST) project seeks to explore how undergraduate research experiences in STEM are shaped by structured mentoring practices. 
}
{
We employed a mixed methods approach, including student interviews and surveys, to examine the efficacy of two mentor practices in particular, daily logs and an intensive "bootcamp" week. 
}
{
After reading through students' interview transcripts and daily logs, bucket codes were developed for Bootcamp and Daily Log mentions and were then consolidated into emerging themes, which were used throughout the qualitative coding process to gather further insights about student experience. Next steps will include analyzing the coded data, exploring the impact of other structured mentoring practices, and developing conclusions about effective mentoring practices. 
}
{
This research is essential for establishing effective mentoring practices that can improve student growth in undergraduate research programs.
}
